# WRPX Jarvis — Feature Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation
Goal: create the private Jarvis app structure.

Build:

- `/jarvis` route.
- Private login protection.
- Dark UI shell.
- Sidebar navigation.
- Home dashboard.
- Basic leads table.
- Manual lead creation.
- Manual stage updates.
- Task/reminder system.
- Jarvis daily briefing placeholder.

This phase should work even before WhatsApp/email integrations are added.

## Phase 2 — Lead pipeline
Goal: make every lead trackable.

Build:

- Lead stages.
- Kanban pipeline.
- Lead detail drawer.
- Notes.
- Photos upload.
- AI summary field.
- Next action field.
- Follow-up due dates.
- Filters by source/stage/status.

## Phase 3 — Jarvis AI core
Goal: make Jarvis useful inside the dashboard.

Build:

- Ask Jarvis text box.
- Jarvis lead summaries.
- Jarvis next-action suggestions.
- Jarvis daily briefing.
- Jarvis task creation.
- Jarvis follow-up drafts.
- Jarvis photo report from uploaded images.

## Phase 4 — WhatsApp and email
Goal: bring conversations into Jarvis.

Build:

- WhatsApp Business Platform integration.
- Inbound message capture.
- Media/photo capture.
- Outbound WhatsApp templates.
- Gmail/email capture if needed.
- Conversation view inside lead record.
- Auto-stage updates based on replies.

Important: the standard WhatsApp Business app alone will not give full automation. Full automation needs the WhatsApp Business Platform / Cloud API.

## Phase 5 — Quotes and invoices
Goal: stop creating documents manually.

Build:

- Quote builder.
- Estimate builder.
- Deposit invoice builder.
- Final invoice builder.
- PDF templates.
- Customer/project variables.
- Quote sent tracking.
- Invoice paid tracking.

## Phase 6 — Pricing and material calculator
Goal: automate pricing support.

Build:

- Measurement input.
- Door/drawer/worktop calculator.
- Material library.
- Extra material rules.
- Cost calculation.
- Margin calculation.
- Suggested price range.
- Material order list.

## Phase 7 — Analytics intelligence
Goal: connect current website analytics to Jarvis.

Build:

- Pull analytics events from existing `/dashboard` data source.
- Create `/jarvis/analytics` page.
- Add Jarvis SEO insights.
- Flag underperforming pages.
- Track pages generating leads.
- Track traffic source quality.
- Recommend content/CTA improvements.

## Phase 8 — Projects and operations
Goal: move from leads to scalable project management.

Build:

- Project records.
- Survey dates.
- Install dates.
- Installer assignment.
- Subcontractor assignment.
- Project notes.
- Job photos.
- Completion checklist.
- Review request automation.

## Phase 9 — Commercial scaling
Goal: support the future WRPX business model.

Build:

- Commercial project type.
- Multi-site jobs.
- Subcontractor database.
- Installer capacity.
- Purchase order fields.
- Commercial quote template.
- Profit by project.
- Profit by client.
- Repeat client tracking.
