## 5 highest-impact fixes

1. **Subcontract window film / manifestation installation page** — A page specifically targeting sign companies, print companies and shopfitters who need installation-only for window film, frosted film or glass manifestation projects. The subcontract geography pages exist but no service-specific subcontract page for film installation. This is a high-commercial-value gap aligned with pillars 1+2.

2. **Gym/leisure window film sector page** — `window-film/gym-window-film/` or `window-film/leisure-window-film/` — a sector hub page for gyms, fitness studios and leisure facilities. All other major sectors (hotel, office, school, restaurant, retail, healthcare, student accommodation, care home) have sector hub pages. Gyms/leisure is missing and is a genuine installation environment with distinct film use-cases (privacy in studio areas, solar control on large glazed frontages, manifestation on glass walls/partitions).

3. **Meeting room / office glass partition privacy film hub page** — `window-film/office-glass-partitions/` or `window-film/meeting-room-privacy-film/` — a dedicated page for the specific buyer problem of frosted film on glass office partitions and meeting room glazing. The existing `frosted-office-film` page covers this broadly, but a more problem-specific page targeting "meeting room privacy film" and "office glass partition frosting" buyer intent would improve coverage for this high-value commercial search cluster.

4. **Retail graphics installation subcontract hub page** — A page specifically for retailers, agencies and print companies needing subcontract installation for retail window graphics, promotional vinyl and seasonal/campaign graphics. Distinct from the geographic subcontract pages — this would target the buyer who has a managed retail rollout and needs a reliable installation partner, not just a local installer.

5. **Internal linking audit on older pages** — Several early architectural wrap pages (hotels-sheffield, offices-sheffield, restaurants-sheffield etc.) lack forward links to newer related pages. A systematic internal link improvement pass across the architectural wrap cluster would improve crawl depth and support rankings for the sub-vertical city pages.
