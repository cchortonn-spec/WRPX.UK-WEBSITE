## Top 5 Highest-Impact Fixes

1. **Core Web Vitals — gallery page** (`/gallery/`): Large unoptimised images likely causing LCP issues. Next.js `<Image>` component with `priority` and explicit `width`/`height` should be used. Estimated +1–2pt lift on Technical SEO.

2. **BreadcrumbList schema on city pages**: Add JSON-LD `BreadcrumbList` to all subcontract city pages and commercial city pages. Improves rich snippet eligibility. Estimated +0.5–1pt lift on Structured Data.

3. **Google Business Profile optimisation**: Cannot action from repo — requires owner to verify/update GBP with consistent NAP, service categories, photos and regular posting. Estimated +1–2pt lift on Local SEO.

4. **Review / testimonial schema**: Add `Review` or `AggregateRating` schema to commercial hub and city pages. Even a single structured review adds eligibility for star snippets. Estimated +0.5pt lift on Structured Data.

5. **Internal linking — older B2B blog posts to new B2B service pages**: Posts like `why-sign-companies-use-subcontract-installers` and `how-to-brief-an-installation-subcontractor` don't link to newer service pages (floor-vinyl-installation-yorkshire, wall-wrap-installation-yorkshire). A pass to add 1–2 contextual links per post would improve crawl depth and distribute authority. Estimated +0.5pt lift on On-page SEO.
