## Top 5 Highest-Impact Fixes

1. **Google Business Profile service categories** — Add "Vinyl Wrapping" and "Window Film Installation" as primary GBP categories. Architectural wrapping pages will not rank well in maps without correct GBP category alignment.

2. **Homepage commercial section** — Add a visible section or card block on the homepage pointing to /architectural-vinyl-film/ and the commercial vertical pages. Currently the homepage is residential-lead; commercial B2B traffic has no clear entry point.

3. **Internal linking from older blog posts** — About 15 B2B blog posts (written July 2026) do not link to the new architectural wrap or window film vertical pages. Adding 2–3 contextual links per post would significantly increase crawl depth and link equity to the new verticals.

4. **Core Web Vitals check on hero images** — The window-film hub page and other pages with full-viewport hero images should be checked in PageSpeed Insights. If LCP is above 2.5s, adding priority={true} and explicit sizes to the Image components is the first fix.

5. **Review count in LocalBusiness schema** — reviewCount: "5" is very low and reduces the impact of the aggregateRating markup. Actively collecting reviews and updating this count would improve rich snippet appearance in local search results.
